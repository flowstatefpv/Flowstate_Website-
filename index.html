Meet the Founder

Flowstate FPV was founded by Erick Salazar, an FPV pilot, engineer, and videographer specializing in creating FPV video and property content.

With a years of hands on flying in FPV freestyle and racing, Erick translates technical piloting precision into cinematic visuals that help brands, properties, and commercial projects stand out.

From high-speed FPV fly-throughs to polished property showcases, Flowstate FPV delivers content designed to make an impression.
