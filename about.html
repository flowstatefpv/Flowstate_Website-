Meet the Team

Founder & Lead Pilot

Erick Salazar

Flowstate FPV was founded by Erick Salazar, a Mechatronics Engineering graduate from Texas A&M. He is the FPV pilot, and videographer for Flowstate FPV specializing in creating FPV video and property content.

With 9 years of hands-on flying in FPV freestyle and racing, Erick translates technical piloting precision into cinematic visuals.

Creative Director

Mariel Salazar

Mariel is Erick's sister and serves as Flowstate FPV's Creative Director. She leads the creative vision behind every project — handling script writing, video editing, and the creative direction of projects.

An Art and Film major at NYU, Mariel brings professional storytelling and post-production expertise that elevates every piece of content we produce.

Sales Manager

Giovanni Cerranto

Giovanni is a UT Tyler graduate holding both a Bachelor's and Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering and currently works as an engineer in Tyler, Texas. His schooling background and engineering profession has built him sharp professional communication skills where he has had to navigate complex client-facing environments.

As Flowstate FPV's Sales Manager, Giovanni brings a client-first approach to every inquiry — helping projects get planned, scoped, and launched smoothly from start to finish.
